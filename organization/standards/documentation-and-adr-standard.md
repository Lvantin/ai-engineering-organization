# Documentation and ADR Standard

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Keep engineering knowledge discoverable and decisions traceable.

## Documentation principles
- Maintain one authoritative location per important fact.
- Prefer concise entry points with links to detailed documents.
- State scope, owner, status, version, and update date for controlled documents.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Update documentation as part of the change that makes it stale.
- Avoid duplicating generated or machine-readable information unnecessarily.

## Architecture Decision Record
Each significant ADR should include:
1. Title and status.
2. Date and decision owner.
3. Context and problem.
4. Constraints and decision drivers.
5. Options considered.
6. Decision.
7. Consequences and trade-offs.
8. Related requirements, contracts, and work items.

## Lifecycle
Proposed → Accepted / Rejected / Superseded. Do not erase historical decisions; link superseding decisions.
