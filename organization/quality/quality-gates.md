# Quality Gates

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Gate 1 — Work Ready
Requirements, scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and validation approach are sufficiently clear.

## Gate 2 — Design Ready
Relevant architecture, data, interface, security, and operational impacts have been assessed.

## Gate 3 — Implementation Complete
Code/configuration changes are complete and required automated checks pass.

## Gate 4 — Review Complete
Code Review is completed; Security Review is completed when applicable; findings are resolved or dispositioned.

## Gate 5 — QA Complete
Required functional and non-functional verification has evidence; defects and limitations are visible.

## Gate 6 — Release Ready
Deployment, migration, monitoring, support, rollback/recovery, UAT, and required human approvals are addressed.

## Gate 7 — Production Verified
Post-deployment checks confirm expected behavior and service health.

A gate may be blocked, passed, or passed with explicitly approved limitations. Agents cannot waive mandatory human approvals.
