# AIEO Quality Model

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Quality dimensions
- Functional correctness.
- Reliability and resilience.
- Performance and scalability.
- Security and privacy.
- Maintainability and evolvability.
- Usability and accessibility.
- Compatibility and interoperability.
- Operability and observability.

## Risk-based application
Not every project needs every test or artifact at maximum depth. The project team must identify applicable dimensions based on business impact, system complexity, data sensitivity, exposure, and operational criticality.

## Evidence
Quality status must be supported by test results, reviews, analysis, or explicit accepted limitations. A green status without evidence is not a valid gate result.
