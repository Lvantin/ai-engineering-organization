# Evidence and Engineering Metrics

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Evidence principles
- Evidence must be relevant to the claim.
- Record commands, environment, version, and outcome where useful.
- Do not claim tests passed if they were not run.
- Distinguish not run, failed, blocked, and passed.
- Avoid exposing secrets or sensitive data in logs and reports.

## Suggested metrics
- Feature lead time.
- Work item cycle time.
- Escaped defects.
- Change failure rate.
- Deployment frequency where meaningful.
- Automated test reliability.
- Review turnaround time.
- Rework rate.
- Security finding age and remediation status.
- Production verification success.

## Responsible use
Metrics are signals for system improvement, not standalone measures of individual performance. Interpret them with context and avoid optimizing one metric at the expense of quality or safety.
