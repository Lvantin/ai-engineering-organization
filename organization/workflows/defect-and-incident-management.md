# Defect and Incident Management Workflow

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Defect handling
Record reproducible behavior, expected behavior, impact, affected version/component, evidence, severity, and reproduction steps. Triage ownership and priority with the appropriate Product/Engineering authority.

## Incident handling
Prioritize safety, service restoration, communication through approved channels, and evidence preservation. Do not expose secrets or sensitive data in incident artifacts.

## Investigation
Use logs, metrics, traces, deployment history, and controlled reproduction. Distinguish confirmed cause from hypothesis.

## Remediation
Define immediate mitigation, permanent fix, regression tests, security implications, and recovery actions.

## Post-incident review
Record timeline, impact, contributing factors, what worked, corrective actions, owners, and due dates. Focus on system improvement rather than blame.
