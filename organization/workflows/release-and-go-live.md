# Release and Go-live Workflow

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Status:** Draft

## Release readiness
Confirm included Features, test results, code/security review outcomes, unresolved defects, migration steps, deployment plan, monitoring, rollback/recovery, support ownership, and release notes.

## Human approvals
- UAT acceptance is provided by an authorized business stakeholder.
- Go-live authorization is provided by an authorized human stakeholder.
- Material residual risk acceptance belongs to an authorized human risk owner.

An Agent may prepare evidence and recommend readiness but must not authorize Go-live.

## Deployment
Follow approved deployment procedures and access controls. Record release identity, environment, operator/automation identity, and outcome.

## Production verification
After deployment, verify critical user journeys, service health, integrations, data integrity, logs, metrics, and alerts as applicable.

## Closure
Record actual outcome, incidents, deviations, rollback/forward-fix actions, and follow-up work.
